Frame it

An instant camera made of your fingers.

  • Hold up both hands like a director's frame. Inside it, the world changes.
  • Tilt the frame like a dial to switch between five lenses.
  • Pinch one hand, keeping the other in its corner, to take the photo. Print the ones you like.

Uses your camera. Nothing leaves this device.
