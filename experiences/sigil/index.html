    Sigil

    Draw magic in the air.

    • Pinch your thumb and index finger to put the nib on the page, and draw in the air.
    • Ink a circle, then a sign inside it: a wave, a triangle, a spiral or a star. Two signs combine.
    • Open your palm toward the circle, hold it, then push to cast.
